## About

I build AI systems that run like enterprise software, not experiments. My focus is local-first AI platforms — models on hardware you own, every action logged and governed, data that never leaves your building.

Before founding West AI Labs, I spent 25+ years shipping production systems at **O'Reilly Auto Parts**, **Bass Pro Shops**, **IBM**, **Bank of America**, and **AIG** — from multi-datacenter infrastructure to enterprise middleware to global financial platforms.

Today I use **MCP** (Model Context Protocol) and **Claude Code** as daily production tools. My AI agent spawns sub-agents, routes to different models by complexity, and writes its findings to files I review. That's not a chatbot — that's infrastructure.
